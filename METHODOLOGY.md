# Methodology

## What these numbers are

`channel-benchmarks.csv` contains the price ranges CryptoTrafficMarket uses when quoting crypto and Web3 marketing campaigns. They are **one agency's working benchmarks**, not a survey of the whole market. Treat them as a practitioner's reference point and compare them with other quotes before budgeting.

`budget-tiers.csv` groups total campaign budgets into five stages, from a market test to an authority campaign. Channel mixes are typical, not fixed.

Ranges show the typical span of what clients paid, not the extremes. Where a range has a single value (for example SEO strategy at 1,100), it is a standard fixed rate.

## What the ranges include and exclude

- Media spend and management fees are listed separately for PPC and banner/native, because clients often buy media themselves.
- PR ranges cover mid-tier crypto media. Tier-1 outlets are excluded; a single placement there usually costs several thousand USD.
- Influencer ranges cover accounts with up to about 100k followers.
- Fees charged directly by platforms (for example paid-priority listing fees) are excluded.
- All figures are in USD, excluding taxes.

## Known limitations

- Prices move with market cycles. In bull markets, influencer and PR prices rise quickly.
- Prices depend strongly on audience quality. Two KOLs with the same follower count can differ in price several times over.
- The data describes what campaigns cost, not what they returned.

## Update policy

The dataset is reviewed quarterly. Each change is logged in `CHANGELOG.md` with its date. If you spot a number that no longer matches the market, open an issue with a source.
